Questions

Good to know

What is a SHA-256 fingerprint?

It is a 64-character code calculated from every byte of a file. The same file always gives the same code. A file that has been edited, cropped or re-saved gives a completely different one. It is the same method banks and software companies use to check files have not been tampered with.

Do I need internet?

Only the first time you open the site. After that, add it to your home screen and it works with no connection at all.

Can someone see my screenshots?

No. The fingerprint is calculated inside your phone's browser. The image is never sent anywhere, and a fingerprint cannot be turned back into the image.

Is this accepted as legal evidence?

Proof Kit helps you show that a screenshot has not been changed since you recorded it, and keeps your evidence organised. It is not a legal certification, and the time comes from your phone's clock. Always keep the original screenshots, and ask the authority you complain to what they need.

What if I clear my browser or change phones?

Your records would be deleted with the browser data. Use "Export CSV" or "Export JSON" in My records to keep a backup somewhere safe, such as with a trusted person.

What does the Quick exit button do?

It immediately replaces this page with a neutral website, in case someone is looking over your shoulder.
